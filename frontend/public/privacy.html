AgileComrade — Privacy Notice

⚠️ MVP / demo software. AgileComrade is an MVP decision-support tool for Scrum Masters and Delivery Managers: it surfaces potential delivery risks early so teams can investigate and act sooner. AI results are signals, not guarantees — they may contain false positives, miss risks, or lack context.

Last updated: August 2026

1. What we store

Your profile lives in Supabase and contains:

  • Connection profile: profile slug, Jira Cloud URL, Jira email, project keys and LLM preferences.
  • Cached sprint snapshot: issue keys, summaries, truncated descriptions (~500 characters), statuses, story points, assignee names, sprint dates, burndown history, and per-sprint scope baselines (issue-key → points maps).
  • Cached AI analyses: generated mitigation plans are stored with the snapshot until regenerated or deleted.

Your Jira API token and LLM API key are encrypted at rest (AES-GCM). Your dashboard access token stays in your browser only — the server keeps just its SHA-256 hash.

Everything above is deleted when you remove the profile in Settings.

3. Data sent to AI providers

When you trigger an analysis we send relevant Jira data: issue keys, summaries, truncated descriptions (~500 characters), statuses, story points, sprint info, and aggregated sprint metrics (burndown gaps, velocity).

  • Assignees are pseudonymized freshly for each request (John Smith → dev-01).
  • Email addresses are scrubbed before analysis.
Important: pseudonymization is not anonymization — names, customer information or other sensitive details may still appear inside free text (summaries/descriptions) and reach your chosen provider. Third-party AI providers have their own data-use, retention and training policies that vary by service, model and plan — this app cannot control or override them. Review your provider's current terms before enabling AI analysis; if your organization forbids sending Jira data to external AI services, leave AI analysis off for that workspace.

4. What we don't do

  • We never write to your Jira workspace — access is strictly read-only. We never create, modify, transition or delete issues.
  • AI-generated follow-up messages are drafts for you to review and copy & paste manually.
  • We don't send real assignee names as identifiers, don't send email addresses, don't sell data, and keep no analytics trackers.
  • We request only the Jira permissions required to read the configured sprint/issue data.

5. AI accuracy & third-party providers

AgileComrade is an MVP, not an authoritative risk-prediction system: flagged items may not be real risks, and real risks may go undetected. Treat results as surface → investigate → apply your delivery judgment → act if warranted. Your professional judgment remains the final authority.

Providers differ in how they handle submitted data (e.g., Gemini's free vs. paid tiers; OpenRouter routing to third-party models with varying retention/training policies — prefer no-training / zero-data-retention options where available for real workspaces).

6. Retention & your controls

  • Edit or delete your profile anytime from ⚙️ Settings — deletion removes the database row and all cached data immediately.
  • AI analysis runs only when you explicitly trigger it; results are returned to your browser and cached with the snapshot until regenerated or deleted.
  • Deleting your profile doesn't retract data already submitted to a third-party AI provider, which remains subject to that provider's retention policies.
