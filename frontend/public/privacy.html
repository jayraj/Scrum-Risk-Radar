Sprint Risk Radar — Privacy Notice

⚠️ MVP / demo software. Sprint Risk Radar is provided as-is for evaluation. Don't connect Jira workspaces containing sensitive or regulated data until a formal data protection agreement is in place.

Last updated: August 2026

1. What we store

  • Your connection profile: profile slug, Jira Cloud URL, your Jira email, project keys and LLM preferences in a single database row (hosted on Supabase).
  • Secrets encrypted: your Jira API token and LLM API key are stored AES-GCM encrypted at rest. Your dashboard access token is kept only in your browser; the server stores just its SHA-256 hash.
  • Cached sprint snapshot: issue keys, summaries, descriptions, statuses, story points, assignee names and sprint dates fetched from your Jira workspace, so dashboards load fast.

2. Where your data goes

3. What never happens

  • The app never writes to your Jira workspace — it is strictly read-only. Follow-up messages are generated for you to copy & paste manually.
  • Real teammate names and email addresses are not sent to AI providers.
  • We don't sell data, add analytics trackers, or require more Jira scope than reading sprints/issues.

4. Retention & your controls

  • Your profile and cached sprint data are kept until you delete the profile in Settings — deletion removes the database row immediately.
  • You can edit or delete your profile anytime from the ⚙️ Settings page.
  • AI analysis happens only when you trigger it; results are returned to your browser.

5. Third-party AI caveat

Even with pseudonymization, prompts may contain your organization's issue titles and text. Data handling differs significantly by provider and plan:

  • Gemini (free tier): Google may use submitted prompts to improve its products. If that is unacceptable for your workspace, use a paid-tier Gemini API key, where submitted data is not used for training.
  • OpenRouter: requests are routed to third-party models with varying retention and training policies. Prefer providers advertising a no-training / zero-data-retention policy, which can be filtered in the OpenRouter dashboard.
  • In all cases, review your provider's current data-use terms before enabling AI features on real workspaces — this app cannot override provider-side retention.
